# Owner-approved Studio X OS logo pack

Use `logo-light-pack-20261104.png` on light surfaces and `logo-dark-pack-20261104.png` on dark surfaces. Both are supplied complete lockups, with a 2048 × 683 aspect ratio. Use `mark-pack-20261104.png` for compact product navigation, the 64 px favicon for browser tabs and the 180 px touch icon for Apple home-screen bookmarks. `app-master-pack-20261104.png` is the supplied 1024 px native launcher master.

These assets were copied directly from the owner-provided StudioXOS-Logo-Pack on 4 November 2026. `START-HERE.txt` records the pack's usage guidance and `ASSET-INDEX.csv` inventories the complete original pack; not every listed original is shipped in this web directory. Monochrome horizontal variants are included for future product-owned material.

Preserve proportions and clear space. Do not recolour, stretch or reconstruct. Keep customer/studio logos on their own websites, client documents and Smart Links. See `docs/BRANDING.md` for the durable product identity rules.
